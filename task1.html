SALAAR

Devaratha "Deva" Raisaar, also known as "Salaar," is the protagonist of the movie "Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire." Played by Prabhas, Deva is a powerful and enigmatic character. He is the exiled prince of the fictional dystopian city-state of Khansaar. The story revolves around his friendship with Varadha, the current prince of Khansaar, and their efforts to confront and overcome the challenges posed by Varadha's father's ministers and relatives2. Deva is depicted as a strong and loyal friend who is willing to go to great lengths to help Varadha become the undisputed ruler of Khansaar. His character is marked by bravery, determination, and a sense of justice
